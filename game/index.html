🌙 MOONLIT DOCK HEIST

A stealth game in a harbour yard at full moon. Slip past the guards, pocket the loot, and reach the glowing exit — unseen.

GOAL: Reach the exit unseen. Guards see in bright amber cones. Crates and walls block their sight. Bushes hide you. Sneak with Shift — walking is loud enough to hear.

Move: WASD or arrow keys · Sneak: hold Shift (slow, quiet, harder to spot) · Hint: H (3 per level, points to the nearest loot) · Pause: P · Restart: R · Sound: M

A guard's suspicion fills when you stand in its cone. Break line of sight and it fades. At full suspicion the guard chases you — if it touches you, the heist is over. Grab all the loot for a perfect score.
